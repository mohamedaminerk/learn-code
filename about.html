about this site

Why this exists

I put my own exercises and projects here, corrected the way I wish someone had corrected mine when I was learning — not just "this is wrong" but why, and what the fix actually changes. Every exercise runs right in the browser, so you can try your own fix before looking at mine.

Everything on this site is a template: the exercises, corrections, courses and projects in assets/data.js are placeholders. Swap them for your own and it becomes your teaching site.
